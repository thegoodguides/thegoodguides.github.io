About Us

We are a non-profit organization with a passion for making positive changes in our communities.

Our Vision

Our Vision

The Good Guides' vision is to work with GTA's vulnerable populations and empower these individuals to make positive lifestyle changes themselves. Our approach will improve our social environment and strengthen the communities we live in as we work towards improving our current youth and promoting the development of a self-reliant youth population.

Our Mission

Our Mission

The Good Guides seeks to develop a positive relationship with at-risk communities by connecting with youth and their families by engaging in social activities. The Good Guides aims to serve our local community by becoming leaders and working with at-risk youth to become self-motivated, empowered, and develop strong social skills to encourage them to reach their full potential. We also aim to foster a therapeutic relationship with these children with a holistic approach to transforming the lives of the communities in which the youth work from within.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

INCLUSIVITY

We believe ALL individuals should have equal opportunity - no youth left behind!

RESPECT

We believe young people must be in a caring and inclusive environment that promotes individuality and reinforces self, social and most importantly, communal respect

EMPOWERMENT

Through mentorship, we aim to empower youth and their families to make positive lifestyle changes

CREATIVITY

We are constantly readjusting our approach within the community in order to be as effective as possible. Society is always evolving and changing, in order to create meaningful change, we will continue to evolve as well

Our Philosophy

Youth on the front lines

The Good Guides believes that the youth should be on the front lines of repairing our communities. No one else can truly understand the trials and tribulations that our children today face than the youth themselves. We believe in placing our youngsters in the driver's seat and providing them with the opportunity and resources to develop new, vibrant, and relevant resources for their communities. Allocating these resources directly back to our community members only increases the effectiveness of our programs and initiatives. This coincidingly provides youth with the opportunity to have a co-op or volunteer placement to gain volunteer hours, school credits, and/or compensation for their work.

Our Programs

Not just a chair at the table — the keys to the entire program.

The Good Guides doesn't invite youth in to watch. We build in tandem: young people create the programs, run the events, and shape the supports — for youth, and for their community. Everything on this page runs on that premise.

The Core Program

Co-op & after-school: the engine everything runs on

Our core program is delivered two ways — through co-op placements and after-school programming. This is where Good Guide Ambassadors come from, and every program, event and support below runs on it.

Co-op & Volunteer Pathway

Co-op Placements — Good Guide Ambassadors

We actively recruit co-op and placement students from both the secondary and post-secondary levels. They join our team as the Good Guide Ambassadors for their respective areas — earning credits toward graduation while they create, develop, and execute real initiatives and events in their own communities.

Ambassadors have the full backing of our organization and access to direct funding, and placements can earn volunteer hours, school credits, and compensation for their work. If you are a student interested in joining our team, don't hesitate to contact us for more information.

Ages 11–18

After-School Programming

After-school programming for young people ages 11–18 — mentoring and support after the school day ends, in the neighbourhoods our youth call home.

And it is built the same way as everything at The Good Guides: in tandem. Not programming done to young people, but programming built with them — for youth, and for the community around them.

How It Works

Built in tandem

Holding the keys means building, not just attending. With The Good Guides, youth co-build in three strands — for youth and their community:

Programs

Ambassadors don't just join programs — they create, develop, and execute them. The Good Guides does not limit our youth to what they can or cannot do throughout the community: we support the individuality, creativity, and passions of youth throughout the GTA, and put the organization's full backing — and direct funding — behind their ideas.

Events

The events in our Gallery were organized by our placement students — Funday With Cops, the Toronto Sports Festival, Woburn Basketball Day, Community Graduation 2K21. Next up: Youth Sports Day on January 30, 2027 — a free sports day and shoe fitting for 300 Scarborough middle-school students.

Supports

Supports built for the whole community, not just the youth in the room: #CareforaHEART hot-meal delivery to neighbours living in homeless encampments, gifts and treat bags handed out in the community, relief for single parents on excursion days — supports our youth identify, organize, and deliver themselves.

The Wider Record

What runs on the core

Built on the co-op and after-school core, the same premise carries into every program we run.

City of Toronto Grant Recipient

Youth Violence Prevention

The Good Guides is a recipient of the City of Toronto's Youth Violence Prevention Grant (2023), delivering de-escalation and violence-prevention programming for youth in priority Scarborough communities, including Eglinton East, Woburn, and Cedarbrae.

Community Engagement

The Good Guides believe that you must always be present to consistently and effectively serve the community. Reliable, innovative, and meaningful engagement will help rebuild trust and relationships between community members and the organization. The Good Guides actively works with many stakeholders to provide community-based engagement, support, and initiatives across the GTA. Our organization strives to deliver the most equitable based care to some of the most neglected parts of the city. Don't hesitate to contact us today if you are interested in supporting a community near you!

Perspective Excursions

The Good Guides works with our youth ambassadors to create, organize and execute various trips. This unique approach will enable our youngsters to witness that anything is possible. Finding an excursion, mapping out the necessary steps and executing will instill vitally essential traits that will translate into all aspects of life. Youth are losing their sense of perspective and forget that the sky is truly the limit regardless of who you are and where you came from. We have told this to youth for ages, but The Good Guides are here to show them!
